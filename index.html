Your League coach,

diff.gg is a Windows app for League of Legends. In champion select it suggests a champion for this draft and builds your items against the enemy team. On the loading screen it tells you who you're really playing against. After the match it explains every death and kill — and Premium opens the exact moment in the replay.

How it works

diff.gg opens by itself at the right moments. You don't have to alt-tab to look for anything.

1 · CHAMPION SELECTPick and plan

The best picks for your role in this draft, then a full build in buying order, Riot's recommended runes, and a short game plan. The build is also added to your in-game shop.

2 · LOADING SCREENKnow who you're facing

For every player: how often they play this role and this champion in their last 20 games, their main role, recent win rate and rank.

3 · AFTER THE MATCHSee your mistakes

A simple report: why each death happened and what to do next time, your kills, fights, build and grade — with a button that plays the moment in the replay.

Features

Everything below works in English and Arabic.

Draft picks Champ select

The three champions of your role that fit this draft best, each with one clear reason:

  • what your team is missing (magic or physical damage, a frontline, crowd control)
  • what answers the enemy (tanks, assassins and dive, squishy teams)

Full build, in order Champ select

Start items, then boots and five items in buying order for your champion against this enemy team, each with its reason: the core, the right boots, and answers to their healers, assassins, CC, tanks and damage type.

Build in your in-game shop

When you lock your champion, the build is saved as a “diff.gg” item set in League's own shop. Follow it in game without leaving the match. Your own item sets are never touched.

Runes, like the client

Riot's recommended rune page for your champion and role, drawn like League's rune tree, with summoner spells. One click saves it as a rune page.

Game plan

Short, practical notes: how to play your lane against your opponent's champion type, the enemy team's biggest threats, and your job in teamfights.

Loading-screen scouting

Each player's games in this role and on this champion out of their last 20, their main role, recent win rate, rank and streak, with tags like “hasn't played jungle in his last 18 games”.

Every death explained

Grouped by cause (ganks, caught alone, fights, 1v1s), then for each death: what happened, what to do next time, and what it cost your team.

Watch the moment Premium

Next to each death and kill: a button that opens League's official replay at 10 seconds before it, with the camera on you, and pauses 5 seconds after.

Kills, fights and players

Your kills (solo kills, multi-kills, shutdowns), every fight on a map with its result, and a score for all ten players with MVP and ACE.

Summary and grade

A four-line summary of the game, your numbers against references for your role, and the three things to work on next — each with what to do.

Rank, LP and your stats

LP gained and lost per game, an LP chart, win rate and average grade per champion, your top three champions for ranked, and a matchup notebook.

All champions

Every champion grouped by main role, with abilities, class, difficulty and damage type.

Only what the data shows

diff.gg never invents a reason. If the recorded data can't support a conclusion, the report says so.

  • 113:00 — a recorded position. Positions are recorded about once a minute.
  • 214:12 — your death. Its exact time and place come from the match events.
  • 315:00 — the next recorded position.

diff.gg only describes what happened between two recordings when both of them support it. Level and gold differences are shown only when they are certain.

What diff.gg won't do

  • No overlay or advice during gameplay, no timers, no automated input. In-game help is limited to the item set you can open in League's own shop.
  • No names, ranks or stats of other players during champion select — Riot keeps champion select anonymous, and so do we. Player info appears only on the loading screen, where names are already visible.
  • No MMR or hidden-rating estimates.
  • No ads inside the game or on the loading screen.
  • No conclusions the recorded data can't support.

Where the data comes from

  • Riot Games API — Account-V1 (Riot ID lookup) and Match-V5 (match list, details and timelines).
  • League Client API (on your PC) — your matches, ranked stats, champion select, rune pages, item sets and the match history used for loading-screen scouting.
  • Live Client Data API (on your PC) — player positions once the game has started, to confirm roles.
  • Replay API (on your PC) — to open a moment in the official replay. It is turned on only after you agree.
  • Data Dragon and League's game data — champion, item and rune names, images and descriptions.
  • The analysis is rule-based and runs on your computer. No match data is sent to any AI service. Our Riot API key stays on our server and is never shipped inside the app.

Privacy & security

  • The Riot ID you enter is sent to our server only to look up your account and your matches.
  • Your reports, notes and settings stay on your computer.
  • diff.gg changes things in League only when you ask or when it's part of a feature you can turn off: the “diff.gg” item set, a “diff.gg” rune page when you click the button, and one line in League's settings to enable replays (a backup of the file is kept).
  • The app's local server only answers your own computer and rejects requests from websites.
  • We don't sell personal data.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything stored on your computer.

FAQ

Is diff.gg allowed by Riot?

diff.gg follows Riot's developer policies (no in-game advantage, champion select anonymity, no MMR calculators) and is registered in Riot's Developer Portal. It uses Riot's official APIs and League's own features such as item sets and replays.

Does it slow down my game?

No. diff.gg doesn't run anything inside the game. During a match it only waits for the game to end.

Which languages?

English and Arabic, for the whole app and every report.

Why can't I watch a moment from an old game?

Riot keeps replays only for the current patch. Older games still have the full written report.

How much will Premium cost?

The price will be announced before launch. Premium adds replay review (watch each death and kill, review sessions) and your full long-term history. Everything else stays free.
