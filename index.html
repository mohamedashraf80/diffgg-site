Understand why you won or lost.

diff.gg is a Windows app that reviews your own League of Legends matches after they end. It explains your deaths, your team's fights and your build, and it tells you plainly when the data isn't enough to be sure.

Only what the data shows

  • 113:00 — a recorded position. Positions are recorded about once a minute.
  • 214:12 — your death. Its exact time and place come from the match events.
  • 315:00 — the next recorded position.

diff.gg only describes what happened between two recordings when both of them support it. Otherwise the report says the data isn't enough.

What you get after each match

Every death explained

What was recorded, what you could have done, and what it cost your team.

Fights

Who was credited in each fight, the result, and whether an objective followed.

Build

Your build compared with the damage and threats you actually faced.

Story of the match

A plain-language timeline of the moments that decided the game.

Your progress

Win rate and average score per champion, and the issue you repeat most.

English and Arabic

The whole app and every report in both languages.

What diff.gg won't do

  • It doesn't run during live games, show overlays, automate input, or give any real-time advantage.
  • It doesn't claim conclusions the recorded data can't support.
  • It doesn't reveal information about other players that isn't already visible to you.

Where the data comes from

  • The official Riot Games API: Account-V1 (Riot ID lookup) and Match-V5 (match list, match details and timelines).
  • Data Dragon for champion and item names, images and descriptions.
  • The analysis is rule-based and runs on your computer. No match data is sent to any AI service.
  • Our Riot API key stays on our server and is never shipped inside the app.

Privacy

  • The Riot ID you enter is sent to our server only to look up your account and your matches.
  • The server keeps a copy of the match data it fetches, so it doesn't request the same match twice.
  • Your reports and settings stay on your computer.
  • We don't sell personal data.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything stored on your computer.
